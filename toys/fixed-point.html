Start with a guess, x₀. Travel vertically to y = cos(x) to calculate the next value, then horizontally to y = x to use it as your new guess. The zigzag closes in on x* ≈ 0.739085, where cos(x*) = x*.

We stop when the residual |cos(xₙ) − xₙ| ≤ 10⁻⁶. All angles are in radians. The little orange line is a cobweb plot. This one comes without spiders.
